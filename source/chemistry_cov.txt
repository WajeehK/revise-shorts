# c-1-3|3.1.3 Bonding|Physical Chemistry
1M|Using its position in the Periodic Table, predict the charge on an ion of sulfur (Group 16).|2−|2+|6+|1−|Sulfur has 6 outer electrons and gains 2 to fill its outer shell, so it forms S²⁻. Group 16 elements form 2− ions.
2M|Magnesium has a high melting point because it has|a giant metallic lattice of Mg²⁺ ions and delocalised electrons|a giant covalent structure|simple molecules held by van der Waals forces|an ionic lattice of Mg²⁺ and Mg²⁻ ions|Strong electrostatic attraction between the positive ions and the delocalised electrons needs a lot of energy to overcome. # c-2-2|3.2.2 Group 2, the alkaline earth metals|Inorganic Chemistry
2W|Explain why the melting point of the Group 2 elements generally decreases down the group.|metallic;delocalised & larger;bigger;radius;size & weaker;less attraction;decrease|The elements have metallic bonding. 2M|In a reaction mechanism, a curly arrow shows|the movement of a pair of electrons, starting from a bond or a lone pair|the movement of a single atom|the direction a molecule moves|the movement of a single electron in a radical reaction|A curly arrow starts at a lone pair or at the middle of a covalent bond (when it breaks) and points to where the electron pair goes.
2W|Why do chemists use mechanisms to explain the reactions of organic compounds?|bonds;electrons & break;broken;form;made;step|A mechanism shows, step by step, which bonds break and form and how the electron pairs move, so it explains why the products form and lets chemists predict similar reactions.
2W|Define the term stereoisomer.|same structural formula & different;same structure & different & arrangement;space;spatial|Stereoisomers have the same structural formula but a different arrangement of the atoms in space. E–Z isomers and optical isomers are both types of stereoisomerism.
2M|But-2-ene shows stereoisomerism (E–Z) because|rotation about the C=C bond is restricted and each carbon of the C=C has two different groups|it has a chiral carbon|it has a branched chain|it has a functional group isomer|The π bond prevents rotation about C=C.
