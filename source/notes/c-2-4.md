# Reactions with oxygen
- Na → Na₂O (yellow flame); Mg → MgO (white flame); Al → Al₂O₃; Si → SiO₂; P → P₄O₁₀ (white flame); S → SO₂ (blue flame).
# Oxides
- Na₂O, MgO and Al₂O₃ are giant ionic, with high melting points. SiO₂ is giant covalent, with a very high melting point. P₄O₁₀ and SO₂ are simple molecular, with low melting points.
- With water: Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH (pH 12–14). MgO gives pH about 9. Al₂O₃ and SiO₂ are insoluble. P₄O₁₀ + 6H₂O → 4H₃PO₄ (pH 0–1). SO₂ + H₂O → H₂SO₃; SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄.
# Acid–base behaviour
- Basic oxides: Na₂O and MgO react with acids. Amphoteric: Al₂O₃ reacts with both acids and alkalis (Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH + 3H₂O → 2NaAl(OH)₄).
- Acidic oxides: SiO₂ (with hot concentrated NaOH, forming Na₂SiO₃), P₄O₁₀ (forming Na₃PO₄), SO₂ and SO₃.
