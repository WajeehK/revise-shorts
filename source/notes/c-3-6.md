# Tests for functional groups
- Alkene: bromine water decolourised. Aldehyde: Tollens' or Fehling's. Carboxylic acid: NaHCO₃ fizzes (CO₂).
- Primary or secondary alcohol: acidified dichromate turns green. Halogenoalkane: NaOH(aq), then HNO₃, then AgNO₃.
# Mass spectrometry
- The M⁺ peak gives $M_r$. High-resolution MS (to 4 d.p.) distinguishes compounds with the same integer $M_r$.
# Infrared spectroscopy
- Bonds absorb IR at characteristic wavenumbers: O–H (alcohol) 3230–3550 broad; O–H (acid) 2500–3000 very broad; C=O 1680–1750; C–H 2850–3300.
- Fingerprint region: below 1500 cm⁻¹, used to match a compound to a known spectrum.
- Greenhouse gases (CO₂, H₂O, CH₄) absorb IR, which contributes to global warming.
# Exam tips
> Quote the **bond** and the **range** from the data sheet when you identify an IR peak.
