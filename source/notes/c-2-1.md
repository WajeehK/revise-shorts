# Periodicity
- Elements are arranged by proton number into s, p, d and f blocks. Period 3: Na and Mg are s-block; Al to Ar are p-block.
- **Atomic radius** decreases across a period: nuclear charge increases while shielding stays similar.
- **First IE** increases across a period, with dips at Al (3p electron, higher energy, more shielded) and S (pair repulsion in a 3p orbital).
# Melting points across Period 3
- Na < Mg < Al: metallic bonding gets stronger (more delocalised electrons, smaller ions with higher charge).
- Si is highest: giant covalent, so many strong covalent bonds must break.
- P₄, S₈, Cl₂, Ar: simple molecular, with weak van der Waals forces. S₈ > P₄ > Cl₂ > Ar because bigger molecules have more electrons.
# Exam tips
> Explain trends using **charge, distance and shielding** for IE and radius, and the **type and strength of bonding** for melting points.
