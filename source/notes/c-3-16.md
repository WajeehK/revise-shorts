# Chromatography
- Separation depends on the balance between solubility in the **mobile phase** and retention by the **stationary phase**.
$$R_f=\frac{\text{distance moved by spot}}{\text{distance moved by solvent front}}$$
- **TLC:** silica or alumina on a plate. Locate spots with UV or iodine (or ninhydrin for amino acids). Compare Rf with known values under the same conditions.
- **Column chromatography:** a column packed with silica; the solvent (eluent) carries the components through at different rates. It is used to purify.
- **Gas chromatography:** the mobile phase is an unreactive gas. Retention time identifies a component and peak area gives its relative amount. It can be coupled to MS (GC–MS).

# Practical tips
- Draw the start line in pencil, keep it above the solvent level, cover the tank with a lid, and mark the solvent front straight away.
# Exam tips
> Rf has no units and is always less than 1. If two spots have similar Rf values, rerun with a different solvent.
