# The mole
$$n=\frac{m}{M_r}$$
$$n=c\times V(\text{dm}^3)$$
$$pV=nRT$$
- $N_A=6.022\times10^{23}\text{ mol}^{-1}$. For $pV=nRT$, use Pa, m³ and K (1 cm³ $=10^{-6}$ m³).
# Formulae and yields
- Empirical formula: divide % by $A_r$, then by the smallest, then scale to whole numbers.
$$\%\text{ yield}=\frac{\text{actual}}{\text{theoretical}}\times100$$
$$\%\text{ AE}=\frac{M_r(\text{desired})}{\sum M_r(\text{products})}\times100$$
- Water of crystallisation: heat to constant mass.
# Titrations
- Concordant titres agree within 0.10 cm³. Rinse the burette with the solution it will hold, and the pipette with the solution it will deliver.
# Worked example
25.0 cm³ of NaOH needs 22.50 cm³ of 0.100 mol dm⁻³ H₂SO₄. Moles of acid $=2.25\times10^{-3}$, so NaOH $=4.50\times10^{-3}$ mol (2 : 1), and $[\text{NaOH}]=0.180\text{ mol dm}^{-3}$.
# Exam tips
> Use the mole ratio from the **balanced** equation, and convert cm³ to dm³ by dividing by 1000.
