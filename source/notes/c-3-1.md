# Nomenclature and formulae
- Formula types: molecular, empirical, structural, displayed, skeletal. General formulae: alkanes CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, alkenes CₙH₂ₙ.
- Naming: find the longest chain, number from the end giving the lowest locants, and list substituents alphabetically (2-chloro-3-methylbutane).
# Isomerism
- **Structural isomers:** chain, position and functional-group isomers (e.g. propanal and propanone).
- **Stereoisomers:** E/Z isomerism needs restricted rotation about C=C and two different groups on each carbon.
- CIP rules: the atom with the higher atomic number has priority. Z means the priority groups are on the same side; E means opposite sides.
# Reaction types
- Homolytic fission gives radicals. Heterolytic fission gives ions.
- Curly arrows show the movement of an electron pair, from a bond or lone pair to an atom or bond.
# Exam tips
> Use skeletal formulae carefully: every corner and end of a line is a carbon, and H atoms on carbon are not shown.
