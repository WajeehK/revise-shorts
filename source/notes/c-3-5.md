# Making ethanol
- **Hydration of ethene:** steam, H₃PO₄ catalyst, 300 °C, 60–70 atm. Fast and pure, but uses finite crude oil.
- **Fermentation:** C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂, with yeast at 35 °C and no oxygen. Renewable, but slow, and the product needs distilling.
- Bioethanol is called carbon-neutral, though in practice farming and transport release CO₂.
# Oxidation (acidified K₂Cr₂O₇, orange → green)
- Primary: aldehyde (distil it off as it forms), then carboxylic acid (reflux, excess oxidant).
- Secondary: ketone. Tertiary: not oxidised.
- Tests: Tollens' gives a silver mirror and Fehling's gives a brick-red precipitate with aldehydes, but not with ketones.
# Elimination
- Dehydration with concentrated H₃PO₄ or H₂SO₄ gives an alkene and water. This makes alkenes from renewable sources.
# Exam tips
> Use [O] in oxidation equations: CH₃CH₂OH + [O] → CH₃CHO + H₂O.
